TBM Soil Conditioning · EPB Shield

IS Series Foam Agents

IS-C · IS-D · IS-P

Three formulations of soil conditioning foam agent for EPB (Earth Pressure Balance) shield TBMs. Mixed and foamed through the machine's foaming device before injection into the cutterhead excavation chamber, where they condition the excavated soil to improve its flow characteristics through the screw conveyor and earth chamber.

All IS Series variants create temporary cohesion in the excavated material, reduce water permeability at the face, provide an air cushion to compensate for abrupt pressure changes, and improve overall muck rheology. The three variants differ in solid content and active chemistry to suit different ground types — from standard sandy clay through dispersive soils to cohesive or mixed-face conditions.

Performance — All Variants

Key Benefits

Reduces clay adhesion & muck cake on cutterhead — foam injection prevents buildup of sticky material on disc cutters and scraper tools, reducing cleaning interventions and clogging risk.
Improves soil rheology — conditioned muck flows more uniformly through the screw conveyor, reducing torque peaks and equipment wear during extraction.
Reduces face permeability — air-entrained foam reduces the hydraulic conductivity of the excavated face, preventing groundwater inflow and improving face support stability.
Prevents soil consolidation — temporary cohesion created by foam prevents granular soils from consolidating in the earth chamber, avoiding plug formation and drive interruptions.
Improves face stability & EPB pressure control — foam compressibility provides buffering against sudden pressure changes at the face, improving EPB pressure regulation during ring building.
Non-toxic to environment (IS-D & IS-P) — biodegradable active agents minimise environmental impact. IS-C is water soluble and biodegradable (>80% in 28 days) — handle with appropriate PPE.

IS-C Safety Information
⚠ GHS Hazard Classification — IS-C Only
IS-C carries GHS hazard classifications.
Read before use.

IS-C (Standard Foam Agent) is classified under the GHS system. IS-D and IS-P do not carry these classifications. All personnel handling IS-C must read the full Safety Data Sheet and wear appropriate personal protective equipment before use.

DANGER
Hazard Statements (IS-C)
  • H302 — Harmful if swallowed
  • H315 — Causes skin irritation
  • H318 — Causes serious eye damage
Required PPE — IS-C
  • Safety goggles / face shield (eye protection)
  • Chemical-resistant gloves (hand protection)
  • Respiratory protection in enclosed spaces
  • Chemical-resistant protective clothing
Transport classification: IS-C is not regulated for transport under ADR, IATA, or IMDG — no hazmat shipping requirements apply.
