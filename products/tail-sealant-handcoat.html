Tail Sealant Hand-coat

Model PA

A dedicated first-fill sealant for the tail sealing system of shield boring machines. Unlike pump-type greases used during continuous driving, PA is manually packed directly into the tail sealing brushes before the initial TBM launch or after a tail brush replacement.

Once in position, PA prevents synchronous grouting slurry and groundwater or soil from infiltrating the tail brush assembly and causing hardening failure. Its fiber-rich homogeneous paste structure ensures uniform distribution throughout the brush cavity, with strong retention under the mechanical contact of advancing segments. The formulation is flame retardant, resisting ignition, flow, and dripping under heat — a critical safety feature during tail brush replacement operations in confined tunnel spaces.

Performance

Key Benefits

Long-term shear stability in wet environments — fiber structure maintains integrity under sustained grout and water pressure without losing cohesion or position within the brush.
Easy & uniform brush filling with good retention — paste consistency allows complete manual packing into brush cavities with minimal voids, and retention resists displacement during ring advance.
Strong adhesion, cohesion & fiber structural support — adhesive to metal shield surfaces and cohesive within the brush cavity; fiber network provides mechanical reinforcement.
High puncture resistance & exclusion capability — resists perforation by aggregate particles in grout while excluding groundwater and fine soils from penetrating the brush assembly.
Flame retardant — resists ignition, flow and dripping under heat. Reduces fire risk during tail brush replacement and maintenance operations inside the tunnel.
Complementary to pump-type tail seal grease — used as the initial fill with pump-type products (e.g. TM-T100) maintaining the seal during continuous driving operations.
