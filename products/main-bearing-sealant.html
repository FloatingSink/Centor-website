Main Bearing Sealant

Model CTR-MBS-P

Clay-thickened lubricating grease formulated specifically for the lips sealing system of TBM main bearings. Based on mineral oil and polymers, it contains solids, fibers and graphite to create an effective barrier against soil, water and dust ingress through the main bearing sealing system.

The product is delivered via the TBM's centralised pump system directly to the sealing lips. Its fiber structure maintains cohesion under wet, high-pressure conditions while remaining pumpable across the full range of site temperatures. Biodegradable and self-extinguishing — it meets EU environmental and fire safety requirements without hazardous classification.

Performance

Key Benefits

Excellent metal adhesion — maintains a tenacious film on sealing surfaces, preventing gap formation under dynamic loading and vibration.
High water resistance — fiber and solid content resists washout from groundwater infiltration and pressurised water ingress at the seal face.
Excellent pumpability — consistent flow at 30–60 g/min through centralised pump distribution systems across a wide temperature range.
Anti-washout with fiber cohesion — fiber structure maintains product integrity and prevents displacement even under continuous water exposure.
Self-extinguishing — automatically extinguishes when ignition source is removed, reducing fire risk in enclosed tunnel environments.
Biodegradable & safe — not classified as dangerous under EU CLP. Safe for groundwater and soil. Simplifies environmental compliance documentation.
