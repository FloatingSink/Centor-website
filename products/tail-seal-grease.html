Tail Seal Grease

Model TM-T100

An economical driving-grade tail sealant formulated for shielded TBMs. TM-T100 is pumped directly into the tail sealing chamber through the machine's automated distribution system, establishing a continuous barrier that prevents ingress of groundwater, soil particles, and annulus grout or mortar grout back into the TBM invert.

The formulation maintains seal integrity at sustained water pressures up to 35 bar and provides strong adhesion to both metal shield surfaces and concrete tunnel lining segments. Its fibrous paste structure offers excellent anti-washout characteristics, preventing product displacement during tail brush contact. Self-extinguishing and flame retardant — not classified as hazardous under EU CLP.

Performance

Key Benefits

Resistant to water, grout & slurry — maintains an effective barrier against all three common intrusion media in the tail void environment simultaneously.
Excellent anti-washout — vertical wash-out ≤40 mm; fibrous structure retains product position even under sustained water flow and contact.
Adhesion to metal & concrete — bonds reliably to both shield steel and precast lining segments, maintaining a continuous seal at the ring-shield interface.
High tail seal brush protection — adequate malleability (≥87%) prevents brush damage while providing sufficient stiffness to resist grouting pressure.
High pressure capacity — tested to 35 bar with no leakage, suitable for deep urban metro and high water table projects.
Self-extinguishing & water insoluble — flame retardant for tunnel safety; water insoluble to prevent environmental leaching into groundwater.
