Wenyi West Road Extension — Phase II

Urban Road Tunnel · 4 Shield Drives

CENTOR provides tail sealant grease and a complete TBM lubrication package across four large-diameter shield drives on the second phase of the Wenyi West Road extension in Hangzhou. Machine diameters on the project range from 11.3 m to 14.5 m — placing these among the largest road-tunnel shields operating in eastern China — and the supply scope is structured to keep all four machines running at sustained advance rates without lubrication-related interruption.
