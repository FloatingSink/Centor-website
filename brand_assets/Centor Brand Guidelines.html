03 Logo System

The master logo

The large segmented “C” symbol with the CENTOR wordmark. Use for website, corporate profile, product packaging, exhibition booths and marketing materials.

CENTOR
Symbol meaningThe segmented C ring represents a tunnel segment ring, the TBM excavation process, system integration, engineering continuity and circular improvement.
Official coloursTop / upper-right / bottom segments — Titanium Silver. Back segments — Industrial Dark Grey. Wordmark — Metallic Silver with a 3D embossed effect.
Clear spaceMinimum clear space equals the height of the “C”. No other graphic element may enter this area.
Minimum sizePrint 25 mm width · Digital 120 px width.

06 Visual Style

Industrial premium, minimal engineering

Industrial PremiumTechnical Authority Underground SystemsEngineering Excellence Minimal Engineering

● Do

  • Lead with a large logo
  • Keep text minimal
  • Build a strong hierarchy
  • Use premium materials
  • Hold clean, open layouts

● Don't

  • Use too many colours
  • Look like a chemical company
  • Look like a construction contractor
  • Use consumer-product branding
  • Over-decorate with graphics
