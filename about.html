01Who We Are

Precision Chemistry for the World Beneath

CENTOR was founded to address a critical gap in the underground construction industry: underground machinery was being lubricated with generic, surface-grade products that were never designed for the extreme pressures, temperatures, and contamination environments found beneath the ground.

Our founders brought together decades of combined underground project experience — from EPB metro drives in Asia to hard-rock TBMs in Europe — to develop purpose-built formulations that address the real challenges of tunnelling operations.

Today, CENTOR operates globally from our Singapore headquarters, supplying specialist products to contractors on some of the world's most challenging underground projects.
